| 2026-10-19 | Epic 3 homepage: deliberate differences from the approved mockup | — | (1) The hero chip "100% Natural / No artificial colours" is now "Made in Egypt / 10th of Ramadan Industrial City": the blanket claim is not substantiated for every product (only tomato paste). (2) The four value-strip descriptions are taken from the official copy instead of mockup text. (3) Stats are counted from the database (4 brands, 42 products, 3 packaging formats), with no "35+" style figures. (4) The hero cycles 3 published slides (max 3, per US-008.5), not 5. (5) Brand/accent colors are adjusted automatically where needed for WCAG contrast (text on orange uses dark ink, orange headline text is darkened); eyebrow green is darker. (6) Brand cards link to `/brands/[slug]` and the product strip to `/products/[slug]`; those pages arrive in Epic 4, so the links 404 on staging until then. |
| 2026-10-20 | Epic 4 brand and product pages: choices beyond the mockups | — | (1) Product specification rows with no data (shelf life, storage, ingredients) are hidden, not shown as "to be supplied" as in the mockup: the epic says empty fields are hidden. (2) Filters are plain links and a GET form, so they work on the server through the URL and without JavaScript; search by name was kept from the mockup. (3) Products of a Coming Soon brand are not listed anywhere; the brand page shows a notice. (4) Old or differently-cased slugs get a permanent redirect (HTTP 308, the modern equivalent of 301). (5) New table `SlugRedirect` records renamed slugs; the admin (Epic 8) will write to it. (6) The Arabic text of the Freshness tagline "More Flavor in Every Drop" (نكهة أكثر في كل قطرة) is our own translation and needs the client's Arabic review. (7) The "Request spec sheet" button of the mockup is left out until spec sheets exist (Epic 8, R2). |

| 2026-10-24 | Epic 5 company and legal pages | — | (1) About, Quality and Innovation are three pages sharing the about.html look; the tab bar shows only these three because Certifications has nothing to show until certificates exist (R2, US-005.6), and no certification name appears anywhere.
